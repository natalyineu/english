Speaking — Practice Paper 7

50 marks · 13 minutes total · 4 parts · Topic: Health & Lifestyle

How to use this page: Practise with a partner (one plays the interlocutor, one the candidate), or record yourself speaking. Click any question to highlight it. Aim for natural, fluent responses — not memorised scripts.
Part 1 Personal Questions 3 minutes
3 min The interlocutor asks your name, spelling, country of origin, then chooses up to 5 questions — one from each topic below.

The interlocutor starts: "Hello. My name's [name]. Can you spell your surname for me, please?" then "Where are you from?" — then selects one question from each topic.

Health
  • ▸How do you try to stay healthy?
  • ▸Do you think people today are healthier than in the past?
  • ▸What do you think is the most important thing for good health?
Sleep
  • ▸How many hours of sleep do you usually get?
  • ▸Do you think most people get enough sleep?
  • ▸Have you ever had problems sleeping? What did you do?
Food & Diet
  • ▸Do you try to eat a balanced diet?
  • ▸Have your eating habits changed in recent years?
  • ▸What do you think about vegetarian or vegan diets?
Stress
  • ▸What kinds of things cause you stress?
  • ▸How do you deal with stress?
  • ▸Do you think modern life is more stressful than in the past?
Free Time
  • ▸How do you relax after a busy week?
  • ▸Do you think people have enough free time today?
  • ▸What would you do if you had a whole free week to yourself?
Part 2 Role-plays 3 minutes
3 min Two role-play scenarios. In Scenario 1 Candidate A initiates; in Scenario 2 Candidate B initiates.
Scenario 1 — A initiates
Doctor's check-up
Candidate A: you are the doctor asking about lifestyle and health concerns.
Candidate B: you are a patient who has been feeling tired and stressed lately.
"So, what brings you in today?"
Scenario 2 — B initiates
B starts Joining a gym together
Candidate B: you are keen to start exercising regularly and want your friend to join you.
Candidate A: you are not sure if you have enough time or motivation.
"I've decided I really need to get fitter — would you consider coming to the gym with me?"
Part 3 Discussion 4 minutes
4 min Discuss the points on your task sheet together and reach decisions. You have 20 seconds to read before starting.

Task sheet:

How can our workplace promote better health and wellbeing?
Situation: Your workplace is launching a wellbeing programme. Discuss the options below and decide which would have the greatest positive impact on employees.
▸Providing healthy food options in the canteen
▸Offering subsidised gym memberships
▸Introducing regular breaks for physical activity
▸Running mental health awareness workshops
Useful language: "I think we should…" · "What about…?" · "I'd prefer… because…" · "Could we compromise on…?" · "That's a good point, but…" · "Let's decide on…"
Part 4 Monologue 3 minutes
3 min 30 seconds to make notes → speak for up to 2 minutes → follow-up questions from interlocutor.

The interlocutor gives you one of the topic cards below. You have 30 seconds to prepare, then speak for up to 2 minutes. The interlocutor will then ask follow-up questions.

Card 1
The importance of exercise
Follow-up questions
  • How often do you exercise?
  • What type of exercise do you prefer?
  • Do you think schools should do more to encourage physical activity?
Card 2
Work-life balance
Follow-up questions
  • Do you think it is important to have a good work-life balance?
  • What do you do to separate work from your personal life?
  • Do you think technology makes it harder or easier to switch off from work?
Monologue tips: Organise into: introduction → 2–3 main points → conclusion. Use connectors: "First of all…", "Another important point is…", "To sum up…". Aim for 2 full minutes — don't rush. Examiners reward range and fluency, not perfection.
