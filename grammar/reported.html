📖 Key Rules — Reported Speech (click to expand)
DirectReported (backshift)
present simple → past simple
present continuous → past continuous
present perfect → past perfect
past simple → past perfect
will → would
can → could
may → might

Time expressions:

now → then  |  today → that day  |  yesterday → the day before  |  tomorrow → the next day / the following day  |  here → there

Common reporting verbs:

say (no object)  |  tell + object  |  ask (if/whether for yes/no questions)  |  admit / deny / suggest + -ing  |  promise / offer / refuse / agree + to-infinitive

⚠️ say + (that) but tell + person + (that). Never: "She told that…"
