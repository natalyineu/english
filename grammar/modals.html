📖 Key Rules — Modal Verbs (click to expand)
ModalUseExample
mustObligation (from speaker), strong deductionYou must wear a seatbelt. / She must be tired.
mustn'tProhibitionYou mustn't smoke here.
have toObligation (external rule)I have to wear a uniform at work.
don't have toNo obligation (not necessary)You don't have to come – it's optional.
should / ought toAdvice, recommendationYou should see a doctor.
might / may / couldPossibility (present/future)It might rain later.
can't / couldn'tImpossibility / inabilityThat can't be right.
couldPast ability, polite request, suggestionShe could swim at age five.
wouldPolite request, conditional, past habit (with always)Would you like some tea?
used toPast habit or state (no longer true)I used to play tennis every weekend.

⚠️ Modals take a bare infinitive: must go, NOT must to go.
⚠️ used to is not a modal – it is followed by the infinitive and can be used in questions/negatives: Did you use to…? / I didn't use to…
