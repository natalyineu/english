Defining relative clauses

Identify which person/thing we mean. No commas. Can omit pronoun when it is the object: The film (that) I watched was brilliant.

Non-defining relative clauses

Add extra information. Always use commas. Cannot use that. Cannot omit the pronoun.

My sister, who lives in Paris, is a chef.    The report, which took months to write, was rejected.

⚠️ Never use both the relative pronoun AND a personal pronoun: "The man who he called me" → "The man who called me".
