📖 Key Rules — Perfect Tenses (click to expand)
TenseStructureKey words / uses
Present perfecthave / has + past participlejust, already, yet, ever, never, since, for, recently, so far · life experiences · past action with present result
Present perfect continuoushave / has been + -ingEmphasises duration or ongoing activity: I've been waiting for an hour.
Past perfecthad + past participleEvent completed before another past event: by the time, before, after, when, already, just
Future perfectwill have + past participleAction completed before a future point: by tomorrow, by the time, by 2030

Present perfect: I have just finished. / She hasn't arrived yet. / We've lived here since 2010 / for ten years.

Past perfect: When he arrived, everyone had already left. / By the time she called, I had eaten.

Future perfect: By next June, she will have graduated. / I will have finished by 5 p.m.

⚠️ Use for with a period of time (for three years), since with a point in time (since 2010).
