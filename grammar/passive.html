📖 Key Rules — Passive Voice (click to expand)
TensePassive structureExample
Present simpleam/is/are + past participleThe letter is written every week.
Past simplewas/were + past participleThe bridge was built in 1890.
Present continuousam/is/are being + past participleThe report is being written now.
Past continuouswas/were being + past participleThe road was being repaired.
Present perfecthas/have been + past participleA decision has been made.
Past perfecthad been + past participleThe car had been stolen.
Future simplewill be + past participleResults will be announced soon.
Modalmodal + be + past participleIt must be done today.

⚠️ Use by + agent only when the agent is important or unknown. Omit it when obvious or unimportant.
