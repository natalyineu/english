🎯 Key Strategies
👁Preview the questions first. Before you listen, read through the questions or gaps carefully. This tells you what to listen for.
✏️Write as you listen. Don't wait until the end — make notes the moment you hear the answer.
🔤Spelling counts. In gap-fill tasks, incorrect spelling means you lose the mark, so check your answers carefully.
🔄Use the second listen. In the real exam, each recording is played twice. Use the first listen for key ideas, the second for detail.
🎭Identify the context quickly. Speakers, situation, and tone tell you a lot. Tune in in the first 10 seconds.
❌Don't leave blanks. There is no negative marking, so always write something — even an educated guess.
