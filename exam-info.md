# LanguageCert International ESOL — B2 Communicator

> **Source:** https://www.languagecert.org/en/language-exams/english/languagecert-international-esol/b2-communicator-2281

## What B2 Means

At B2 level you can:
- Communicate in detail in everyday, travel, and work situations
- Spontaneously interact with fluency without strain on either party
- Follow news broadcasts and understand complex texts
- Write correspondence, reports, and creative narratives
- Function independently in English-speaking environments

---

## Written Exam — 2h 40min

### Listening — 30 minutes

| Part | Task | Questions |
|------|------|:---------:|
| 1 | Multiple choice — 7 unfinished conversations (including 1 formal) | 7 |
| 2 | Multiple choice — identify topic, purpose, speaker relationships | 6 |
| 3 | Note-taking — write specific info in **1–5 words** | 7 |
| 4 | Multiple choice — discussions (gist, opinions, cause/effect) | 6 |

**Total: 26 questions**

---

### Reading — ~1h 5min

| Part | Task | Questions |
|------|------|:---------:|
| 1 | Multiple choice — long text (news article, review, or proposal) | 6 |
| 2 | Gap-fill — choose from sentence options (1 distractor included) | 6 |
| 3 | Matching — match statements to 4 short texts | 7 |
| 4 | Short answers (up to **5 words**) — continuous narrative text | 7 |

**Total: 26 questions**

---

### Writing — ~1h 5min

| Part | Task | Length |
|------|------|--------|
| 1 | Formal response (email/letter) for a public audience | 100–150 words |
| 2 | Personal letter, narrative, or descriptive composition | 150–200 words |

---

### Written Exam Scoring

| Result | Score |
|--------|-------|
| High Pass | 101–150 / 150 |
| Pass | 75–100 / 150 |
| Fail | 0–74 / 150 |

**Pass mark: 50% (75/150)**

## Spoken Exam — 13 minutes

Format: face-to-face or online with a live interlocutor.

| Part | Task |
|------|------|
| 1 | Give name/spelling, country of origin + answer 5 general questions |
| 2 | Respond to 2–3 situations — initiate and maintain interactions |
| 3 | Collaborative discussion — make plans or decisions using written prompts |
| 4 | 30s preparation → 2-min monologue on a topic → follow-up questions |

### Spoken Exam Scoring

| Result | Score |
|--------|-------|
| High Pass | 38–50 / 50 |
| Pass | 25–37 / 50 |
| Fail | 0–24 / 50 |

**Pass mark: 50% (25/50)**

## Key Skills to Practise

Based on the exam format, prioritise:

- **Listening:** identifying gist, opinions, cause/effect; short note-taking (1–5 words)
- **Reading:** skimming long texts; gap-fill; matching across multiple texts; short-answer writing
- **Writing:** formal emails/letters (100–150 words); narrative/descriptive writing (150–200 words)
- **Speaking:** spontaneous responses; structured 2-min monologue; collaborative discussion
